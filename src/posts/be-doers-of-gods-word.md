# Be Doers of God's Word

I keep coming back to this thought: there's a difference between hearing God's Word and actually living it.

Some of us are hearers. We listen, we take notes, we sit with the message. And then there are doers. They allow what they hear to shape how they live.

In Matthew 7:24-27, Jesus talks about two foundations. Hearing His words builds a strong foundation, but hearing without acting leads to ruin. 

So what are we supposed to do with what we hear?

## Show Your Love Through Action

Romans 5:8 reminds me that Jesus showed His love through action. He didn't just say it, He demonstrated it. And in 1 Corinthians 13:4-7, love is described in everyday simple terms: patience, kindness, endurance.

If love doesn't show up in how I treat people, then maybe I'm still just listening.

## Show Your Light Through Action

Jesus says we are the light of the world.

That means our faith should be visible. Ephesians 5:9 talks about living in goodness, righteousness, and truth. Being a good example is one of the quiet ways light shines.

It's less about saying the right things and more about living them.

## Show Repentance Through Actions

Acts 26:20 talks about repentance that's proven by deeds. Turning to God should lead to a change direction. It should be something others can see. Not for attention, but because real change leaves evidence.

## Show Your Love Through Prayer

Prayer is also an action.

Choosing to pray every day, even when nothing feels urgent, is an act of love and obedience. It's showing up consistently, not just when I need something.

I don't wnat to be someone who only hears God's Word.

I want to be someone who lives it.
